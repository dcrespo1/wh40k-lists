Game sequence

11th Edition

Pre-game

  • 1. Muster armies: choose Force Disposition (this list: Purge the Foe OR Priority Assets, pick one)
  • 2. Determine mission (from both players' dispositions)
  • 3. Determine deployment map
  • 4. (Optional) Twist
  • 5. Create the battlefield
  • 6. Deploy and finish pre-game, in your mission pack's order:
  • ↳ Declare Deep Strike reserves: any Cthonian Beserks units (Fury from the Dêlve)
  • ↳ Embark the Hekaton (14 slots; Exoarmour and Steeljacks take 2 each)
  • ↳ Hernkyn Yaegirs: Infiltrate (>8" from enemy deployment zone and enemy units)
  • ↳ Start of battle: Hostile Acquisition, 0 YP

Each turn

  • 1. Start of turn step
  • 2. Command: +1 CP each → start-of-phase → Battle-shock → END: earn YP → Memnyr gain/spend → (your turn) 7+ YP = Fortify Takeover, else Hostile Acquisition
  • 3. Movement: Augmented Assault → move → reinforcements (Beserks Deep Strike, Dêlvewerke Navigator)
  • 4. Shooting (cover = -1 BS)
  • 5. Charge (chargers get Fights First until end of turn)
  • 6. Fight: Fights First combats, then the rest, alternating
  • 7. End of turn: non-mission rules, then mission scoring

Planning rules

  • One stratagem per unit per phase
  • YP are earned at the end of BOTH players' Command phases; your state is only checked at the end of yours
  • Deep Strike: >8" from enemies. Rapid Ingress: not in battle round 1
  • Ingress: wholly within 6" of an edge, >8" from enemies; not in enemy deployment zone before round 3

Game plan

Purge the Foe or Priority Assets (recommended: Purge the Foe)

Shoot from the Hekaton and Hearthguard, drop three Beserk blocks on whatever's left, and feed YP from objectives into the stratagems that make each hit land.

Which disposition?

  • Recommended: Purge the Foe. Three Deep Strike Beserks units, Hearthguard plasma and the Hekaton are built to kill, and Kâhl earns YP for each unit his squad destroys.
  • Priority Assets fits the YP engine too: Hearthkyn sticky objectives (Luck Has), Memnyr's YP per objective held, and Fortify Takeover's -1 to wound when you sit on points.
  • Pick Priority Assets instead if you expect a tough opponent you can't out-kill, and lean on holding points and Fortify Takeover.

Deployment

  • Hearthkyn Warriors (OC 20 each) on home objectives: home objectives earn YP every Command phase, and Luck Has keeps them yours if you leave.
  • Memnyr + Steeljacks (13 slots) in the Hekaton, or near a midfield objective: Computational Mastermind gives YP for each objective he holds.
  • Kâhl + Hearthguard (21 slots) won't fit in the Hekaton. Deploy them within 9" lines of approach for Avatars of the Ancestors.
  • Beserks units in Deep Strike; Berehk's unit is the big hit. Kâhl (Dêlvewerke Navigator) should see where they land.
  • Yaegirs Infiltrate forward to screen; Pragmatic Hunters lets them step away when enemies close in.
  • Buri alone on a flank: Lone Operative and Stealth keep him safe from long-range fire.

Scoring

  • Purge the Foe (destroying units): Kâhl gains 1YP each time his unit kills a unit. Hostile Acquisition's +1 to hit vs units on objectives fits this plan.
  • Priority Assets (holding high-value points) leans on Hearthkyn, Memnyr and Fortify Takeover instead. See Matchups.
  • From round 2, holding 1 and then 2 objectives outside your zone gives +1 YP each, and +1 more if your opponent holds fewer.
  • Fortify Takeover (7+ YP) is the defensive state. Spending below 7 on Cyberstimm Infusion or Fury of the Hearth for a big kill is usually worth it in Purge the Foe.
  • Hidden Accessways (end of opponent's Fight) lifts a Beserks/Hearthkyn/Yaegirs unit out to re-drop on a new objective.

Dêlve Assault Shift + Hearthguard Covenant · 1995/2000 pts · Purge the Foe or Priority Assets

Don't forget

Start of battle: Hostile Acquisition, 0 YP. Beserks in Deep Strike

End of EACH player's Command phase: Earn YP: 1 for a home objective; from round 2, +1 for 1 outside, +1 for 2 outside, +1 if opponent holds fewer

End of your Command phase: Memnyr: gain or spend 1 YP for each objective he holds · Hearthkyn: objective they're on becomes sticky (Luck Has) · Check state: 7+ YP = Fortify Takeover, otherwise Hostile Acquisition

Your Movement phase: Augmented Assault: spend up to 2 YP on Beserks (+Move, charge after Advance) · Reinforcements: Dêlvewerke Navigator spends YP to bring Beserks models back

Shooting and Fight: Fury of the Hearth (Hearthguard: +1 S, 1 YP for Sustained Hits 1) · Cyberstimm Infusion (Beserks: re-roll wounds, 2 YP for full re-rolls) · Kâhl's unit kills a unit: +1 YP (once per phase)

Opponent's turn: Yaegirs: D6" move when an enemy ends a move within 8" · Buri: D6+2" surge after being wounded by shooting · Weavewërke Buttress (Hostile only) / Brëkkeknots (Hearthguard 4+ invulnerable) · Overwatch with Steeljacks (hit on 5+, 4+ in Fortify)

Before the battle

  • Cthonian Beserks units (all three) — may start in Deep Strike reserves
  • Hernkyn Yaegirs — Infiltrators: deploy >8" from enemy deployment zone and enemy units
  • Army — starts with Hostile Acquisition and 0 YP

Command phase

  • Army — END of each player's Command phase: earn YP from objectives (see Reminders)
  • Memnyr Strategist — end of YOUR Command, before the state check: gain or spend 1 YP per objective he holds
  • Hearthkyn Warriors — end of your Command: an objective they hold stays yours until the opponent out-controls it at a phase end
  • Army — end of your Command: 7+ YP = Fortify Takeover (+1 hit on your controlled objective; -1 to wound vs your non-vehicle units when S beats T), else Hostile Acquisition (+1 hit vs units on objectives; re-roll Advance and Charge)
  • Strat: Insane Bravery (1CP) — auto-pass a Battle-shock test (once per battle)

Movement phase

  • Strat: Augmented Assault (1CP) — a Beserks unit not yet moved: spend up to 2 YP for +1" Move each, and it can charge after Advancing
  • Reinforcements — Beserks Deep Strike >8" from enemies
  • Kâhl — Dêlvewerke Navigator (reinforcements step): spend YP to return destroyed models to a Beserks unit he can see
  • Memnyr — Multiwave System Jammer (once per battle): treat the round as one higher when a Cthonian unit arrives from Reserves

Shooting phase

  • Hearthguard — re-roll hit 1s vs the closest target; Avatars: re-roll wound 1s vs targets within 9"
  • Strat: Fury of the Hearth (1CP) — Hearthguard +1 S on ranged attacks; spend 1 YP for Sustained Hits 1
  • Hekaton — ignores all BS and hit roll modifiers (Damaged, cover) if you want; re-roll hit 1s; Conversion beamer crits on 4+ vs targets beyond 12"
  • Hearthkyn Warriors — re-roll hit 1s
  • Strat: Explosives (1CP) — Hearthkyn or Yaegirs (Grenades): six D6 vs enemy within 8", each 4+ = 1 MW

Charge phase

  • Hostile Acquisition — re-roll Charge rolls
  • Strat: Crushing Impact (1CP) — Hekaton after a charge: roll 12 D6, 5+ = MW to enemy (max 6), 1s hurt you

Fight phase

  • Strat: Cyberstimm Infusion (1CP) — a Beserks unit re-rolls wound 1s; spend 2 YP to re-roll any wound roll
  • Strat: Unstoppable Force (1CP) — a Votann unit Piles In / Consolidates 6" instead of 3"
  • Beserks — Cyberstimms: a model killed before it fights stays to fight on 4+ (3+ in Fortify Takeover)
  • Kâhl — when his unit destroys an enemy unit: +1 YP (once per phase, any phase)
  • Strat: Epic Challenge (1CP) — a Character's melee gets Precision

Opponent's turn (reactive)

  • Yaegirs — Pragmatic Hunters: enemy ends a move within 8" → Yaegirs make a D6" Normal move (not if engaged)
  • Strat: Fire Overwatch (1CP) — end of their Movement; Steeljacks hit on 5+ (4+ in Fortify); Memnyr makes it 1CP cheaper once per round
  • Strat: Rapid Ingress (1CP) — end of their Movement, not round 1
  • Buri — Unhinged Vengeance: after an enemy unit shoots and wounds him, surge up to D6+2"
  • Strat: Weavewërke Buttress (1CP) — Hostile Acquisition only: an infantry unit being shot gets -1 to wound vs it
  • Strat: Brëkkeknots (1CP) — Hearthguard targeted (shooting or either Fight): 4+ invulnerable save
  • Strat: Heroic Intervention — Berehk's unit costs 1CP less and ignores other uses that phase
  • Strat: Counter-offensive (2CP) — their Fight phase: your unit fights next
  • Strat: Hidden Accessways (1CP) — end of their Fight: unengaged Beserks/Hearthkyn/Yaegirs go to Strategic Reserves

Always on

  • Beserks + Berehk — FNP 4+; Hearthkyn — FNP 6+ and 5+ invulnerable (Weavefield Crest)
  • Hearthguard — 5+ invulnerable (Rampart Crest and Weavefield Crest; they don't stack)
  • Buri — Lone Operative (only shot from within 12"), Stealth; first death: 2+ to come back at full wounds
  • Steeljacks — on objectives, critical wounds have Precision (Preymark Crest)
  • One stratagem per unit per phase
