Game sequence

11th Edition

Pre-game

  • 1. Muster armies: Force Disposition = Take and Hold. Lion El'Jonson must be your Warlord
  • 2. Determine mission (from both players' dispositions)
  • 3. Determine deployment map
  • 4. (Optional) Twist
  • 5. Create the battlefield
  • 6. Deploy and finish pre-game, in your mission pack's order:
  • ↳ Decide reserves: Lion (Deep Strike); Belial + Knights (Deep Strike, or Strategic Reserves to use the Teleport Homer)
  • ↳ Embark: Rhinos carry up to 12 non-Terminator infantry each (see Rhino card)
  • ↳ Before the battle: place Belial's Teleport Homer token

Each turn

  • Start of battle round: +1 CP (Lion's Transhuman Strategist)
  • 1. Start of turn step
  • 2. Command: +1 CP each → pick combat doctrine; Lion picks 2 Primarch abilities + Master Strategist → Battle-shock step
  • 3. Movement: Azrael names a unit + doctrine; Techmarine blesses a vehicle → then reinforcements
  • 4. Shooting (cover = -1 BS; Bolter Discipline near objectives)
  • 5. Charge (chargers get Fights First until end of turn)
  • 6. Fight: Fights First (Lion always), then the rest, alternating
  • 7. End of turn: non-mission rules, then mission scoring

Planning rules

  • One stratagem per unit per phase
  • Each doctrine once per battle (Assault twice): only 4 doctrine picks in total
  • A unit has only one doctrine at a time, except the Lion (Master Strategist adds one on top)
  • Deep Strike: >8" from enemies. Rapid Ingress: not in battle round 1
  • Ingress: wholly within 6" of an edge, >8" from enemies; not in enemy deployment zone before round 3
Steps from the 11th Edition core rules; follow your mission pack where noted.

Game plan

Take and Hold

Hold the middle with tough, hard-to-wound infantry, kill the enemy units that contest it with the Lion and Deathwing, and time your four doctrine picks for the turns you charge.

Deployment

  • Intercessors (OC 10 each) on home objectives: Bolter Discipline gives them +1 to hit while an objective is in range anyway.
  • Gladiator Lancer and Ballistus on long firing lanes; Techmarine within 3" of one of them (Lone Operative there, and the Blessing next Movement).
  • Rhino A: Azrael + Companions (7). Rhino B: Hellblasters + Ezekiel (6), or an Intercessor squad. Rhinos can't carry the Knights (Terminators).
  • Lion: deploy within 3" of a Dark Angels infantry unit for Lone Operative, or keep him in Deep Strike.
  • Belial's Teleport Homer goes in midfield cover where enemies won't walk within 1" of it.

Scoring

  • Biggest OC: Azrael's unit 13, Intercessors 10 each, Belial's unit 6, Hellblasters 5, Lion and Ballistus 4 each.
  • Lion's Will (1CP, either Command phase): +1 OC on a unit already on an objective. In Sacrifice, Victory (1CP): when a Deathwing unit dies on an objective, keep it secured.
  • Doctrine timing: you get Assault twice, then Devastator and Tactical once each. Save Assault for the rounds you charge onto the midfield.
  • Intercessors are your action-doers: Tactical Mainstay lets them act while engaged or battle-shocked and still shoot.
  • Save Insane Bravery (once per battle) for Azrael's unit or an Intercessor squad holding a key objective.

Before the battle

  • Lion — must be your Warlord (Supreme Commander)
  • Belial + Knights — place one Teleport Homer token anywhere (once per battle)
  • Lion, Belial + Knights — may start in Deep Strike reserves

Start of each battle round

  • Lion (Warlord) — Transhuman Strategist: gain 1 CP

Command phase

  • Army — pick one combat doctrine for every unit: Assault (charge after Advancing), Devastator (ranged weapons get Assault), Tactical (shoot and charge after Falling Back)
  • Lion — pick 2 of: Mist-wreathed Shadow Realms (go to Strategic Reserves, ingress this turn's Movement, even round 1), Martial Exemplar (6" aura: melee re-roll hit and wound 1s), No Hiding From the Watchers (6" aura: FNP 5+ vs psychic and mortal wounds)
  • Lion — Master Strategist: one extra doctrine for him only, on top of the army's
  • Strat: Tactical Mastery (1CP) — a Deathwing unit gets its own doctrine until your next Command phase
  • Strat: Lion's Will (1CP, either turn) — unit in range of an objective gets +1 OC until end of turn
  • Strat: Insane Bravery (1CP) — auto-pass a Battle-shock test (once per battle)

Movement phase

  • Azrael — Masterful Tactician: name one Adeptus Astartes unit within 9" he can see and a doctrine; it replaces that unit's doctrine until your next Command phase
  • Techmarine — start or end of his move: a vehicle within 3" heals D3 and can ignore hit/wound modifiers until your next Movement phase
  • Lion — ingress move if he used Mist-wreathed Shadow Realms
  • Reinforcements — Deep Strike >8" from enemies

Shooting phase

  • Intercessors — Bolter Discipline: +1 to hit if an objective is in range of them or of the target
  • Ballistus — Sustained Hits 1 vs targets within 24"
  • Gladiator Lancer — vs Monsters/Vehicles: re-roll one hit, one wound and one damage roll
  • Ezekiel — Engulfing Fear (psychic level 1): roll D6 (1 = he's battle-shocked), then an enemy within 12" takes a Battle-shock test at -1
  • Rhinos — Firing Deck 2: two passengers shoot with the Rhino's storm bolter
  • Strat: Relics of the Dark Age (1CP) — an infantry unit's ranged attacks get +2 S
  • Strat: Explosives (1CP) — Explosives unit, enemy within 8": six D6, each 4+ = 1 mortal wound

Charge phase

  • Assault doctrine — units that Advanced can still charge
  • Strat: Crushing Impact (1CP) — Lion, Ballistus or Lancer after a charge: roll D6 equal to its T, 5+ = MW to enemy (max 6), 1s hurt you

Fight phase

  • Lion — always Fights First
  • Azrael's unit — +1 to hit vs Characters; Belial's unit — +1 to wound vs Characters
  • Strat: Gene-wrought Might (1CP) — melee gets Lance, plus +1 AP if the unit has Assault doctrine
  • Strat: Inescapable Justice (1CP) — melee gets Precision
  • Strat: Duty in Death (1CP, when enemy targets your unit) — dying models stay to fight on 4+ (+1 with Assault doctrine)
  • Belial — killed before his unit fights: 2+ and he still fights

Opponent's turn (reactive)

  • Strat: Rapid Ingress (1CP; 0CP with Teleport Homer) — end of their Movement, not round 1; Belial's unit arrives within 3" of the token, >8" from enemies
  • Strat: Fire Overwatch (1CP) — end of their Movement: snap-shoot, hit on 6s
  • Strat: Smokescreen (1CP) — start of their Shooting: Rhino or Lancer gets cover; Rhinos -1 CP and ignore other uses
  • Strat: Armour of Contempt (1CP) — when an enemy shoots/fights your unit: its attacks get 1 worse AP
  • Ezekiel — Whispers of the Shadow Forest (psychic level 1): when targeted, D6 (1 = battle-shocked), attacks vs him -1 to hit this phase
  • Strat: Heroic Intervention (1CP) — end of their Charge: unit within 12" charges (Ballistus qualifies: Walker)
  • Strat: Counter-offensive (2CP) — their Fight phase: your unit fights next

Always on

  • Dutiful Tenacity — attacks with Strength above your infantry's Toughness get -1 to wound
  • Azrael's unit — Stealth; melee attacks vs them -1 to hit; 4+ invulnerable
  • Deathwing Knights — every attack vs them has -1 Damage
  • Lion — -1 to wound if S beats T10; FNP 4+ vs psychic and mortal wounds; Lone Operative within 3" of DA infantry
  • Ezekiel — psyker level 3: psychic abilities used in a battle round add up to at most 3 levels
  • Damaged 4 (Ballistus, Lancer) — -1 to hit at 1-4 wounds left; the Techmarine's Blessing cancels it
  • One stratagem per unit per phase
