# Game sequence (for the order-of-operations card)

Use this to build the "Game sequence" card. Everything under "Confirmed" was
checked against 11th Edition core rules sources (November 2026). Keep the
"follow your mission pack" wording for the steps marked that way rather than
filling them in: the exact order there comes from the Chapter Approved deck.
For a 10th Edition game, say so and give only the five phases.

## Confirmed: pre-game (11th Edition)
1. **Muster armies.** Each player's Detachments unlock Force Dispositions; each
   player picks ONE for the battle (in organised play it's locked in when the
   list is submitted, and picked in secret). Builder exports often show it in the
   header (e.g. "Take and Hold").
2. **Determine mission.** The two players' dispositions together decide the
   missions. Each player plays the one in their own row of the matrix
   (`assets/disposition-matrix.json`), so the two players usually score in
   different ways.
3. **Determine deployment** (deployment map).
4. *(Optional)* **Twist.**
5. **Create the battlefield.**
6. **Deploy and finish pre-game:** follow your mission pack's order (deploying,
   pre-battle abilities such as Scouts or tokens, who goes first).

## Confirmed: each player's turn (11th Edition)
1. **Start of turn step:** resolve "start of turn" rules.
2. **Command phase:** both players gain 1 CP → start-of-phase abilities →
   Battle-shock step. Units at or below half strength test, and units that are
   already battle-shocked test to recover (it no longer wears off on its own).
   A battle-shocked unit has OC "–", can't be targeted by its own stratagems,
   and can't start or complete actions.
3. **Movement phase:** move units → then set up reinforcements.
4. **Shooting phase.** Cover makes attacks against the unit worse by 1 BS.
5. **Charge phase.** Units that charge have Fights First until end of turn.
6. **Fight phase:** Fights First combats first, then remaining combats,
   players alternating.
7. **End of turn step:** non-mission rules first, then mission scoring.

## Confirmed: rules that matter for planning
- Only one stratagem per unit per phase.
- Engagement range: 2" horizontally, 5" vertically.
- Deep Strike: set up more than 8" from all enemy models.
- Ingress move (strategic reserves): wholly within 6" of a battlefield edge and
  more than 8" from enemies; before battle round 3, not in the opponent's
  deployment zone. Rapid Ingress can't be used in battle round 1.

Sheet rules override any of this.
