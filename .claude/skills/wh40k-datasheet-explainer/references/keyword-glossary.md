# Keyword Glossary (fallback)

The sheet's own rule text always wins. Use this only for keywords the sheet names
without defining. Three sections, in order of trust:

1. **Confirmed 11th Edition changes**: safe to state.
2. **10th-Edition-era meanings**: generally still right, but say "check the
   current Core Rules" when the exact effect matters on an 11th Edition sheet.
3. **Do not explain**: keywords whose meaning isn't confirmed. Name them and
   say they aren't defined on the sheet. Never guess.

Sheets mix vocabulary: a faction still on 10th-era datasheets can show [PISTOL]
and GRENADES on an 11th Edition export (as warmind.online's Leagues of Votann
export does), so treat edition clues as hints. Explain Pistol and Close-Quarters
the same way, and Grenades and Explosives the same way.

Spotting an 11th Edition sheet: Close-Quarters instead of Pistol, Explosives
instead of Grenades, "Damaged X" as a named ability, psyker/psychic levels,
Deep Strike at more than 8",
Cleave, Mobile.

A **critical hit / critical wound** is an unmodified 6 on that roll unless
something says otherwise. "S beats T" in builder wording means S is greater than T.

## 1. Confirmed 11th Edition changes
- **Close-Quarters / Pistol:** Close-Quarters is the new name for Pistol. Can shoot while the unit is in
  melee. Can appear on weapons that aren't pistols.
- **Explosives / Grenades:** Explosives is the new name for the Grenades keyword (used by the Explosives
  stratagem).
- **Deep Strike:** set up more than 8" from all enemy models (was 9").
- **Cover:** attacks against the unit get worse by 1 BS (no longer +1 to the save).
- **Engagement range:** 2" horizontally, 5" vertically.
- **Charging:** a unit that charges has Fights First until end of turn.
- **Battle-shock:** test at or below half strength; a shocked unit stays shocked
  until it passes a test in a later Command phase. While shocked: OC "–", can't
  be targeted by its own stratagems, can't start or complete actions.
- **Hazardous:** hazard roll fails on 1–2: 1 mortal wound, or 3 if every model
  in the unit is a MONSTER/VEHICLE.
- **Devastating Wounds:** a critical wound becomes mortal wounds equal to the
  weapon's D, but each critical wound can only kill one model (extra is lost).
- **Lethal Hits:** a critical hit may auto-wound (optional, because an
  auto-wound can't become a critical wound for Devastating Wounds).
- **Ingress move:** see references/game-sequence.md.
- **Stratagems:** only one stratagem per unit per phase.
- **Damaged X:** when the model has 1–X wounds left, the sheet states the
  penalty (usually −1 to hit).
- **Psyker level / psychic level:** a psyker can use psychic abilities whose
  levels add up to no more than its psyker level each battle round. Psychic
  rolls (usually a D6, 1 = battle-shocked) are explained on the sheet.

## 2. 10th-Edition-era meanings (check current wording when exactness matters)

### Stat line
- **M (Move):** inches the unit moves.
- **T (Toughness):** compare to weapon Strength. S ≥ 2×T wounds on 2+,
  S > T on 3+, S = T on 4+, S < T on 5+, S ≤ half T on 6+.
- **Sv (Save):** armor save. Weapon AP worsens it.
- **Invulnerable save:** a save AP can't worsen. Use whichever is better.
- **W (Wounds):** damage each model can take.
- **Ld (Leadership):** Battle-shock tests, roll 2D6 and need at least Ld.
- **OC (Objective Control):** how much the unit counts toward holding objectives.
- **FNP (Feel No Pain):** roll for each wound lost; on the value or higher it's
  ignored. An asterisk usually means it only applies to some attacks (the
  ability text says which).

### Weapon keywords
- **Assault:** can shoot after Advancing.
- **Heavy:** +1 to hit if the unit didn't move.
- **Rapid Fire X:** X extra attacks within half range.
- **Torrent:** hits automatically.
- **Blast X:** extra attacks against large units; can't fire into a melee
  your units are in.
- **Sustained Hits X:** critical hits score X extra hits.
- **Anti-[KEYWORD] X+:** vs that keyword, a wound roll of X+ is a critical wound.
- **Twin-linked:** re-roll wound rolls.
- **Melta X:** +X Damage within half range.
- **Lance:** +1 to wound on a turn the bearer charged.
- **Ignores Cover:** target gets no cover.
- **Indirect Fire:** can shoot unseen targets at a penalty; target gets cover.
- **Precision:** can target an attached Character in the unit.
- **Extra Attacks:** used in addition to another melee weapon.
- **One Shot:** once per battle.
- **Psychic:** marks a psychic attack (matters for some defensive rules).

### Core abilities
- **Scouts X":** free move before the game.
- **Infiltrators:** deploy anywhere more than 9" from the enemy.
- **Stealth:** ranged attacks against it get −1 to hit.
- **Lone Operative:** can only be shot from close range unless near friends;
  the condition is usually on the sheet.
- **Fights First:** fights before units without it.
- **Deadly Demise X:** on death, roll a D6; on a 6, X mortal wounds to units nearby.
- **Firing Deck X:** X passengers can shoot from the transport.
- **Leader:** joins one of the listed Bodyguard units.

## 3. Do not explain (meaning not confirmed)
- **Cleave X** (seen on melee weapons, e.g. Lion El'Jonson's Fealty sweep)
- **Mobile** (unit keyword)

When the user tells you what one of these does, use their explanation for the
rest of the conversation and suggest they add it to this file.
